# Mote

A quiet place for thoughts.

Mote is an offline-first note-taking app built as a Progressive Web App. It runs entirely on your device — no accounts, no servers, no telemetry. Everything you write stays in IndexedDB on the phone or computer you typed it on.

---

## What it does

- **Notes** — free-form writing, markdown-friendly, with a live preview.
- **Lists** — structured checklists. To-dos, bucket lists, shopping lists.
- **Tags** — organize across notes, filter the list with one tap.
- **Search** — full-text across titles, bodies, tags, and list items.
- **Sort** — recently edited, recently created, or A → Z.
- **Trash** — soft-deleted notes you can restore or purge forever.
- **Offline** — installable, works fully without a network.
- **Dark mode** — light, dark, or follow the system.

No sign-in. No sync. No subscriptions. Just notes.
